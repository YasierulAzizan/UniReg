## Recording tips

- Record in one Google Meet or Zoom call with screen sharing, one person sharing the slides. Each speaker turns their camera on for their part.
- Do one practice run with a timer. If it runs over 14 minutes, cut words, not slides.
- A 20-second live demo of UniReg (log in, register, see the clash message) is a strong opener for Amir's part, but only if it fits the time.
- Use one slide template and one colour scheme for everyone. Yasierul's slides are purple; match them or ask to restyle them.
- Export the recording as MP4 and check the sound before submitting.
